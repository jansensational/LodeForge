- **Update notice:** it now lists what the new version brings.

## v0.1.1 · 24 Nov 2026

- **Save and reload:** the button on the update notice now always loads the new version, even while GitHub is still serving the old page.

## v0.1.0 · 24 Nov 2026

First numbered release, hosted on GitHub Pages.

- **Version number:** shown in the tab title, the Controls card and the pack, with a notice when a newer version is out.
- **World files:** save your world to a file, load it back, or autosave straight to a file in Chrome or Edge.
- **Ice:** water freezes below 0 °C, and the top of the world is permafrost with frozen lakes. Mine ice for water casks.
- **Steam engine:** it boils water. It needs a pool or water casks, takes a few seconds to raise steam, and vents hot steam.
- **Boiling:** water boils into steam at a boiling point set by the pressure above it, and steam condenses back as it cools.
- **Temperature:** heated gas expands and rises. Fires, candles and vents add heat, and above 60 °C the air scorches. G cycles gas view, heat view and off.
- **Air:** nitrogen, real partial pressures, CO₂ poisoning, toxic oxygen and crushing pressure.
- **Managing the air in your hold:** Glowmoss planters, Gas monitors, catwalks and Rune airlocks.
